# Burghood training video: script

A guided tour of the Burghood app for residents and service providers. It runs under 3 minutes: about 412 words of narration (about 2.3 minutes at a natural pace), plus short chapter title cards. The screens use sample data (Maple Court HOA, Steel City Plumbing); no real people or accounts.

Each line gives what happens on screen and what the voice-over says. The narration is also in `tool/training_video/script.json`, which the recording tools read.

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Steps | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Getting started | 3 | about 20 s |
| 2 | The community map | 2 | about 15 s |
| 3 | Reporting a problem | 5 | about 30 s |
| 4 | Your society | 4 | about 23 s |
| 5 | Finding a provider | 3 | about 20 s |
| 6 | For service providers | 2 | about 16 s |

## 1. Getting started

**Step 1 (a1)**

- **On screen:** Open the app. Tap **Get started**. Pick **Month: June** and a birth **Year**, then tap **Continue**.
- **Narration:** "Welcome to Burghood. The first time you open the app, tap Get started, then choose your month and year of birth, and tap Continue."

**Step 2 (a2)**

- **On screen:** The community map opens. Tap each tab in turn: **Society**, **Report**, **Activity**.
- **Narration:** "Along the bottom are the five main sections: Map, Society, Report, Activity, and Profile."

**Step 3 (a3)**

- **On screen:** Tap **Profile**, then **Sign in**. Type the email and password and tap **Sign in**. "Signed in as Ann Lee" appears.
- **Narration:** "To join your community and follow your reports, sign in from Profile with Google, Apple, or your email address."

## 2. The community map

**Step 4 (b1)**

- **On screen:** On **Map**, tap the **Pothole** chip on, then off. Tap the **list** button (top right).
- **Narration:** "The map shows the problems your neighbors have reported, from potholes to broken streetlights. Use the chips to filter by type, or switch to a list."

**Step 5 (b2)**

- **On screen:** Tap the report "Deep pothole in the right lane…". The details sheet opens. Tap **Me too**.
- **Narration:** "Open a report to see its photo, how serious it is, and who it was sent to. If it affects you too, tap Me too."

## 3. Reporting a problem

**Step 6 (c1)**

- **On screen:** Tap **Report**. On "Where is the problem?" tap **On the street**.
- **Narration:** "To report something, tap Report, and choose On the street."

**Step 7 (c2)**

- **On screen:** Tap **No, continue to report**. Tap **Choose from library** (the pothole photo is added), then **Next**.
- **Narration:** "First, confirm that no one is in danger. If someone could get hurt, Burghood points you to 911. Then add a photo of the problem."

**Step 8 (c3)**

- **On screen:** Tap **Use my location**, then **Next**. Tap **Pothole**, then **Urgent**. Type "Deep pothole in the right lane", then tap **Next**.
- **Narration:** "Set the exact location, choose what it is and how serious it is, and add a short note."

**Step 9 (c4)**

- **On screen:** The review screen shows **Who fixes this?** with the office and its contacts. Scroll, then tap **Submit report**.
- **Narration:** "Burghood shows you who actually fixes it, with their contact details. Tap Submit report."

**Step 10 (c5)**

- **On screen:** Tap **Activity**. Open the new report, tap **Mark fixed**, then **Mark fixed without a photo**.
- **Narration:** "You can follow your report in Activity, and you'll get updates as it moves along. Once it's repaired, open it and tap Mark fixed."

## 4. Your society

**Step 11 (d1)**

- **On screen:** Tap **Society**. Maple Court HOA opens. Tap the society name to show the switcher (Grant Street Lofts), then close it.
- **Narration:** "Society is your community's home. Tap its name to switch if you belong to more than one."

**Step 12 (d2)**

- **On screen:** Tap **General**. React with 👍 on a post. Type "Thanks Raj, I will book a plumber." and tap **Send**. Go back.
- **Narration:** "Channels are group conversations for the whole community. Open one to react, reply, and post your own message."

**Step 13 (d3)**

- **On screen:** Tap the **Notices** tab (the pinned water shut-off notice), then the **Docs** tab (bylaws, budget, minutes).
- **Narration:** "Notices are official announcements from your admins, and Docs keeps your bylaws, budgets and minutes."

**Step 14 (d4)**

- **On screen:** Tap **Manage**. Under join requests, tap **Approve** for Nia Brooks. Scroll to the invite code. Go back.
- **Narration:** "Admins approve new neighbors under Manage, and can share an invite code so others join straight away."

## 5. Finding a provider

**Step 15 (e1)**

- **On screen:** Tap the **Services** tab. In the search box, type "kitchen tap leaking". The **Plumbing** chip appears and results load.
- **Narration:** "In Services, just describe what you need. Type kitchen tap leaking, and Burghood finds plumbers near you."

**Step 16 (e2)**

- **On screen:** Scroll the results: **Sponsored** (Three Rivers Drain Co.), then Steel City Plumbing with **Recommended by your society**.
- **Narration:** "Providers your community recommends come first, background checks are badged, and sponsored listings are clearly marked."

**Step 17 (e3)**

- **On screen:** Tap **Steel City Plumbing** and scroll to the reviews. Tap **Request this provider**, type the title and details, add a photo, and tap **Send request**. The request timeline opens.
- **Narration:** "Open a profile to read reviews from real jobs, then tap Request this provider, describe the job, and add a photo."

## 6. For service providers

**Step 18 (f1)**

- **On screen:** Signed in as Pete (the provider): **Activity › Jobs**, open "Kitchen tap leaking", then **Accept job**, **Start job** and **Mark as done**. Type the note, add a photo and tap **Send to neighbor**.
- **Narration:** "Providers see new work under Activity, in Jobs. They accept the job, start it, and mark it done with a note and photos."

**Step 19 (f2)**

- **On screen:** Signed in as Rosa: **Profile › Offer your services**. Fill in the name, phone and a service, then the area. Tick the provider rules and tap **Send for review**. After approval, the **Listing fee** card with **Pay now** appears.
- **Narration:** "Anyone can offer their services from Profile, under For service providers. Once the Burghood team approves them, the listing fee and how to pay appear right here."

## 7. Closing the loop

**Step 20 (g1)**

- **On screen:** Signed in as Ann: **Activity › Requests**, open "Kitchen tap leaking", tap **Confirm it's done**, choose **5 stars** and tap **Send review**.
- **Narration:** "Back as a resident, open the request, confirm the work is done, and leave a rating for your neighbors."

**Step 21 (g2)**

- **On screen:** Tap **Profile**. Under Settings, tap **Dark**, then **System**. Tap **Larger**, then **Default**.
- **Narration:** "Profile also has your theme, text size and notification settings. That's Burghood: fix your street, look after your home, and know your neighbors."

## Recording notes

- **Voice:** a warm, friendly voice at a natural pace. Pause briefly at each chapter title.
- **Music:** soft, light background music, kept well under the voice and faded in and out.
- **Taps:** each tap shows as a soft circle on screen. Typing appears one character at a time.
- **Captions:** use the narration lines above as the captions.
