Burghood Provider Rules

Effective: [launch date] · Contact: viv.anand@gmail.com

These rules apply to every business or person listed as a provider on Burghood, alongside the terms of use and the community guidelines. By applying, you agree to follow them.

Your application

  • Everything you submit must be true and current: your business name, contact details, categories, service area and documents.
  • Upload a valid licence for any work that needs one in Pennsylvania or your municipality, and proof of liability insurance where your category asks for it.
  • List only categories you are qualified and insured to do.
  • Tell us within 7 days if a licence, insurance policy or clearance expires, is suspended or is revoked. Your listing is paused until a current document is on file.

Personal care: babysitting, pet care and similar

  • Before your listing goes live, upload a current Pennsylvania State Police criminal history check (PATCH).
  • For any work with children, also upload a Pennsylvania child abuse history clearance and, where Pennsylvania law requires it, an FBI fingerprint check.
  • Renew these at least every 60 months, or sooner if the law or the family you work for requires it.
  • Never leave a child or pet in someone else's care without the resident's agreement.

Doing the work

  • Accept or decline a request promptly. Don't accept a job you can't do.
  • Agree the price, scope and timing with the resident before you start. Burghood doesn't set prices or take payment for jobs.
  • Respect people's homes, privacy and property. Use a resident's phone number and address only for their job.
  • Mark a job done only when it is finished. Photos or video of the finished work must be of that job and must not show people's faces or private documents.
  • If a resident marks a job not fixed, respond and put it right, or explain why you can't.

Reviews

  • Don't offer discounts, gifts or anything else in exchange for a review or for changing one.
  • Don't review yourself, a competitor, or a job you were part of.
  • You may reply to a review once, politely and without sharing the resident's personal information.

Fees and sponsored listings

  • When listing fees apply, your listing goes live once your fee is recorded and runs for the period shown in the app. You'll get a reminder before it ends.
  • Fees and sponsorships are paid outside the app and are not refundable once your listing or sponsorship has started, except where the law requires.
  • A sponsored listing is labelled as such and doesn't change your rating or reviews.

Enforcement

We may reject an application, or suspend or remove a listing, if you break these rules, if your documents can't be verified, or if we receive credible reports of unsafe, dishonest or abusive conduct. We'll tell you why. You can reply to viv.anand@gmail.com to ask us to review the decision.

Changes

We may update these rules. We'll let providers know in the app before a change takes effect.
