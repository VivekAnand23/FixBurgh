Burghood Community Guidelines

Effective: [launch date] · Contact: viv.anand@gmail.com

Society channels are for neighbors. These guidelines keep them useful and friendly. They apply to posts, comments, replies, reactions and photos.

Be a good neighbor

  • Be respectful, even when you disagree. Criticize ideas, not people.
  • Keep posts relevant to your society and the channel they're in.
  • Use @mentions to reach someone who needs to see a post, not to pile on.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, bullying, or hateful content about anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, sexual orientation, age or national origin.
  • Sharing someone's private information, such as their phone number, unit, or photos of them or inside their home, without their agreement.
  • Spam, repeated advertising, chain messages or scams. Providers should use their Burghood listing, not channel posts, to advertise.
  • False claims about a neighbor, the society or its management.
  • Sexual or violent content, or anything illegal.

Safety

Channels aren't monitored around the clock. For an emergency, call 911. For urgent building problems, contact your management company directly.

If something is wrong

  • Report a post or comment from its menu. Society admins and Burghood staff review reports.
  • Block someone to stop seeing their posts and comments.
  • Mute a channel if you want fewer notifications.

What happens when guidelines are broken

Society admins can remove posts and comments and remove members from the society. Burghood can also remove content and suspend accounts that break these guidelines or the terms of use.
