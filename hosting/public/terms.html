Not for emergencies

Burghood is not monitored and is not an emergency service. If anyone could be hurt, such as downed power lines, a gas smell, fire, a crash or flooding over a road, call 911.

What Burghood does

Burghood helps you find the office responsible for a problem, share reports with neighbors, talk with your society, and find local service providers. Burghood is an independent community project. It is not run by, and does not speak for, any government agency, utility or authority, and it cannot guarantee that a problem will be fixed or when. Office contact details come from public sources and may be out of date.

Using Burghood safely

  • Never use the app while driving, and never stop in traffic to take a photo.
  • Don't trespass or put yourself at risk to take a photo.
  • You must be 13 or older to use Burghood.

Your content

You are responsible for what you post. Reports must be honest and about public infrastructure. Don't post:

  • false, spam or duplicate reports made on purpose;
  • offensive, harassing or hateful content;
  • other people's personal information, or photos where a person is the subject.

By submitting a report, you allow Burghood to display it publicly on the community map and to share anonymized report data, such as counts by area, to help improve local infrastructure.

Society channels

Channels are for the members of a society. Follow the community guidelines. Society admins can remove posts and comments and manage members; Burghood can also act on anything flagged to us.

Service providers

Providers on Burghood are independent businesses and people. They are not Burghood's employees or agents, and Burghood is not a party to any job, quote or payment between you and a provider. Agree the price and scope of a job with the provider before work starts, and check that they hold any licence the work needs. Burghood reviews the documents providers upload, but we can't guarantee their work, conduct, licence, insurance or background. A “Background checked” badge means a current result was on file when we reviewed it, not that the provider is safe for every situation. Providers must follow the provider rules.

Reviews

You can review a provider once for each job you confirm as done. Reviews must be honest and about that job. Providers can reply but can't remove reviews. We may remove reviews that break these terms.

Sponsored listings

Some providers pay to appear above search results. These listings are always labelled “Sponsored” and kept apart from the other results, which are ranked by rating and distance. Paying for a sponsored listing doesn't change a provider's rating or reviews.

Moderation

Anyone can flag a report. Reports flagged by several people are hidden until reviewed. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these terms. You can hide reports from a specific person in the app.
