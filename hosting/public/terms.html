Not for emergencies

FixBurgh is not monitored and is not an emergency service. If anyone could be hurt, such as downed power lines, a gas smell, fire, a crash or flooding over a road, call 911.

What FixBurgh does

FixBurgh helps you find the office responsible for a problem and lets you share reports with neighbors. FixBurgh is an independent community project. It is not run by, and does not speak for, any government agency, utility or authority, and it cannot guarantee that a problem will be fixed or when. Office contact details come from public sources and may be out of date.

Using FixBurgh safely

  • Never use the app while driving, and never stop in traffic to take a photo.
  • Don't trespass or put yourself at risk to take a photo.
  • You must be 13 or older to use FixBurgh.

Your content

You are responsible for what you post. Reports must be honest and about public infrastructure. Don't post:

  • false, spam or duplicate reports made on purpose;
  • offensive, harassing or hateful content;
  • other people's personal information, or photos where a person is the subject.

By submitting a report, you allow FixBurgh to display it publicly on the community map and to share anonymized report data, such as counts by area, to help improve local infrastructure.

Moderation

Anyone can flag a report. Reports flagged by several people are hidden until reviewed. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these terms. You can hide reports from a specific person in the app.
