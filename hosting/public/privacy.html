Burghood Privacy Policy

Effective: [launch date] · Contact: viv.anand@gmail.com

Burghood is a free app for residents of Allegheny County, Pennsylvania. You can report problems on public streets, talk with your society (an HOA, condo association or apartment community), and find local service providers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your information, and we show no third-party ads. Some provider listings are paid placements, marked “Sponsored”; they use no information about you.

What is public

When you submit a report, these parts are shown to everyone on the community map: the photos, the location you pin, the category, severity and description, the report's status, and the office it was routed to. Your name, email and account are never shown on the map. Before upload, the app removes hidden photo data (such as the GPS and camera details stored inside the image file), and it masks phone numbers and email addresses typed into descriptions. Please don't photograph people's faces, license plates, or the inside of private homes.

Societies

If you join a society (an HOA, condo association or apartment community) on Burghood, its members can see your name as it appears on your account, your unit number and your role. Notices and documents in a society are visible only to its approved members. We store the link to your HOA management company's account page, never balances or payment details.

Verifying society admins

To create a society, you claim its admin role and upload two documents:

  • a government photo ID (driver's license, state ID, passport or passport card, or military ID), and
  • proof that you hold office, such as a board resolution, election minutes or a management agreement that names you.

We collect these only to check that the person creating a society really runs it, so residents don't join fake societies. Only Burghood staff with platform admin access can open them, and each time they do it is recorded. They are not shown to the society, its members or anyone else, and we don't use them for anything else.

The files are deleted automatically 30 days after we approve or reject the claim. We keep a record of the claim itself (the society's name and address, your name, email and position, the decision and who made it) for as long as the society exists, so we can show who verified it. If you delete your account, we delete your claims and any files that are left.

Society channels

Posts, photos, comments, replies and reactions in a society's channels are visible only to that society's approved members. Each shows your name and, on posts, your unit. If you @mention someone, they are notified. You choose which notifications you get, and you can mute any channel. Admins of the society can remove posts and comments. Before upload, the app removes hidden data from photos, as it does for reports.

Service requests and providers

When you post a service request, the provider you choose (or, for a common-area request, your society's admins and the provider they assign) can see its title, description, photos, category details, your name and the address or area you give. Requests are not public. Each step of a job (accepted, started, done, confirmed) is recorded in its timeline.

If you leave a review, your star rating, comment and name are shown publicly on the provider's profile, with the provider's reply if they write one.

If you apply to be a provider, we collect your business name, description, phone number, the categories you offer, your service area, and the documents you upload, such as a licence, proof of insurance and background-check results. Your business name, description, categories, area, ratings, reviews and past job photos are public. Your phone number is shown only to residents whose requests you have accepted. Your documents are never public: only Burghood staff with platform admin access can open them, and each time they do it is recorded.

Background checks

Providers who offer personal care, such as babysitting or pet care, must have a background check and, for work with children, Pennsylvania child abuse clearances before their listing goes live. Burghood does not run these checks itself. The provider gets them from the issuing agency and uploads the results, and our staff record that the check was reviewed and when. A “Background checked” badge means only that a current result was on file when we reviewed it; it is not a guarantee.

Payments

Providers pay a listing fee, and businesses can pay for sponsored placements. These payments are made outside the app. Burghood staff record each one (who paid, the amount, the date, the method and a reference) so we can keep accounts and prepare audit reports. We never collect card or bank details in the app. Payment records can't be edited or deleted; a correction is recorded as a separate reversing entry.

When you contact an office

When you tap Call, Email or Web form, Burghood opens your phone, email app or browser. The agency receives only what you send it. Burghood does not send reports to agencies for you.

Children

Burghood is not for children under 13. We ask for a birth month and year before you can use the app, and anyone under 13 cannot continue. We store only whether the check passed. If you believe a child under 13 has used Burghood, contact us and we will delete the account and its data.

Who processes data

Burghood runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, crash reporting and analytics) and Google Maps. Data is stored in the United States. These providers process data only to run the service.

How long we keep data

  • Reports stay on the map while open. Fixed reports leave the default map after 30 days and remain in the reporter's history.
  • Deleted reports and their photos are removed right away.
  • Society claim files (photo ID and proof of office) are deleted 30 days after the claim is decided.
  • Channel posts and comments stay until their author or a society admin deletes them, or the society is removed.
  • Service requests and their timelines stay in the history of the resident and provider involved. Reviews stay on the provider's profile; if the reviewer deletes their account, the review stays without their name.
  • Provider documents stay while the provider's profile exists, so we can confirm a licence, insurance or background check is still current.
  • Payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is deleted.
  • When you delete your account, your account data is removed within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete any of your reports from the report's detail screen.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete my account. You can also request deletion on the web.
  • Turn off location access in your phone's settings and place the pin by hand instead.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and let users know in the app.
