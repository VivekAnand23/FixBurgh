FixBurgh Privacy Policy

Effective: [DATE] · Contact: [SUPPORT_EMAIL]

FixBurgh is a free app for reporting local infrastructure problems in Allegheny County, Pennsylvania. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your information, and FixBurgh has no ads.

What is public

When you submit a report, these parts are shown to everyone on the community map: the photos, the location you pin, the category, severity and description, the report's status, and the office it was routed to. Your name, email and account are never shown on the map. Before upload, the app removes hidden photo data (such as the GPS and camera details stored inside the image file), and it masks phone numbers and email addresses typed into descriptions. Please don't photograph people's faces, license plates, or the inside of private homes.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Photos, pinned location, category, severity and description of a reportTo show the problem on the map and route it to the responsible office
Your device's location, only while you use the appTo place the pin. We store only the pin you confirm. We never collect your location in the background.
Account details: a random user ID for guests; your email and name from Google or email sign-in if you choose to sign inTo keep your report history and limit spam
Votes ("Me too", "Looks fixed") and flags you submitTo reduce duplicates, confirm fixes and moderate content
Whether you confirmed you are 13 or older (not your birth date)To comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
Crash reports and basic usage statistics (no photos, locations or text)To fix bugs and improve the app

When you contact an office

When you tap Call, Email or Web form, FixBurgh opens your phone, email app or browser. The agency receives only what you send it. FixBurgh does not send reports to agencies for you.

Children

FixBurgh is not for children under 13. We ask for a birth month and year before you can use the app, and anyone under 13 cannot continue. We store only whether the check passed. If you believe a child under 13 has used FixBurgh, contact us and we will delete the account and its data.

Who processes data

FixBurgh runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, crash reporting and analytics) and Google Maps. Data is stored in the United States. These providers process data only to run the service.

How long we keep data

  • Reports stay on the map while open. Fixed reports leave the default map after 30 days and remain in the reporter's history.
  • Deleted reports and their photos are removed right away.
  • When you delete your account, your account data is removed within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete any of your reports from the report's detail screen.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete my account. You can also request deletion on the web.
  • Turn off location access in your phone's settings and place the pin by hand instead.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and let users know in the app.
