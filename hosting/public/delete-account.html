Delete your FixBurgh account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FixBurgh and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Choose what happens to your reports: keep them on the map with no link to you (the problems still exist), or delete them and their photos.
  4. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email address you signed in with, using the subject "Delete my FixBurgh account". We will confirm and delete your account and its reports within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (user ID, email and name).
  • Your reports and photos if you choose to delete them (or all of them if you ask by email). Reports you choose to keep stay on the map with nothing linking them to you.
  • Your "Me too" and "Looks fixed" votes.

Anonymous, aggregated counts, such as the number of reports in a municipality, may be kept.
