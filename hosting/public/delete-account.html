Delete your FixBurgh account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FixBurgh and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. To delete individual reports first, open each one from My Reports and tap Delete report.

Without the app

Email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email address you signed in with, using the subject "Delete my FixBurgh account". We will confirm and delete your account and its reports within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (user ID, email and name).
  • The reports and photos you choose to delete, or all of them if you ask by email.
  • Your "Me too" and "Looks fixed" votes.

Anonymous, aggregated counts, such as the number of reports in a municipality, may be kept.
